Description

FINAL PROFIT is a story driven management shop-sim RPG hybrid with depth and density that will take you to unexpected places as it gradually unfolds and evolves.

  • (As of the 1.2.3 update, now also includes Just Shop Mode! An alternate roguelike shop keeping game mode!)

With over 30 original songs and 35+ hours of gameplay.

Your Fortune, Your Way

  • Gather products and find customers.

  • Invest in stocks, commodities, and real estate.

  • Almost every building in the game can be purchased, adding to your passive income.

Choices and Consequences

  • Many decisions lead to branching outcomes that change shop systems, customer behavior, and product interactions. With both short and long term effects.

  • Deep upgrades that interconnect.

  • Master a system, then automate it.

Rewarding Exploration

  • Learn spells that interact with the world.

  • Discover puzzles, minigames, collectables, and rich lore.

  • Secrets around every corner.

Get Sidetracked: The Game

“I’ve sold all my stock and made a tidy profit! Time to head out and pick up some more, but wait… There was that customer I wanted to find… And that area that was blocked off, I just learned the spell to open it up! And I’ve saved up enough for an upgrade, but which one to get first? Oh! Is that a chest I missed? Now I have enough Apples to venture deeper into the Apple Labyrinth! (Just remember to pick up that stock on the way back!)”

Play as the fierce former Queen of the Fae

Exiled by your own daughter because of your radical plan to save the realm from financial predators by beating them at their own game.

The Fae Council thinks you have no chance of taking down the Bureau of Business.

Prove them wrong.

Starting with nothing

Without the support of your kingdom you’ll enter the world of business and try to carry out your plan. Build your fortune until you become a Lord of Business!

But you'll need more than just money

Seek out allies to help overcome the danger, traps, and colossal debt the Bureau has in store for you. There’s bound to be others who wish to change the status quo.

From simple beginnings in a small town

Learn the ropes in a little shop while getting introduced to the world and characters. Once you get the hang of things you’ll be ready to move to a bigger city and tackle more complex problems. Keep growing your fortune and influence until you reach your goal!

Confronting Capitalism

  • Will you emerge with your morals intact and save your people?

  • Or will you compromise and become a ruthless agent of the system you swore to dismantle?
