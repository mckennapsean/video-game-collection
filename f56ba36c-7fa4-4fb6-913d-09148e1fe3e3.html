Description

YOUR ULTIMATE HOBBY

Celebrate 15 years of the ultimate railway hobby! Learn to operate officially licensed locomotives from historic steam to modern day electrics and explore realistic detailed routes from around the world. Train Simulator Classic 2024 is the simulation made by railfans for railfans.

EXPLORE FAMOUS RAILWAYS

Journey to five countries and take a tour of over 450 km of rails. Explore the picturesque North Wales Coast Line in the UK. Travel through the Saxony countryside in Germany with Riesa – Dresden. Head to the USA capital and ride the bustling Northeast Corridor. Race through breathtaking scenery in France with Marseille - Avignon. And discover the stunning Alpine landscape of Austria with Mittenwaldbahn.

LEARN OFFICIAL LOCOMOTIVES

Officially licensed, authentic and detailed - Climb aboard 12 locomotives in Train Simulator Classic 2024. Push the throttle to experience high-speed with the Amtrak Acela® I, Avanti ‘Super Voyager’ or SNCF TGV Duplex. Keep to time on regional passenger services with locos including the BR Class 158 'Sprinter', ACS-64, and DB BR 442 ‘Talent 2’. Deliver heavy freight with the versatile DB BR 189 and the ÖBB 2016 ‘Hercules’.

CREATE YOUR JOURNEY

Use the Train Simulator Editor to build living worlds with comprehensive tools. Create your own dream railway or scenario and start a service with your favourite locomotive. With full Steam Workshop support you can share your creations with other railfans around the world, or browse through the thousands of community creations already available to try out.
