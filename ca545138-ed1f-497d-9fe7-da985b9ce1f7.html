Description

​

Step into a miniature world where toy trains chug through nostalgic environments like living rooms, couch forts, attics, and picnic blankets- all lovingly crafted as cozy dioramas filled with nostalgic charm. Teeny Tiny Trains is a laid-back puzzle game that captures the wonder of childhood playrooms and the satisfying click of a well-placed track.

As the clever conductor of your own teeny railway, you’ll solve bite-sized puzzles by laying down tracks, navigating whimsical obstacles, and guiding your trains to their destinations. Each level is a handcrafted brain teaser designed to spark joy, reward experimentation, and make you smile.

  • Charming Track-Building Puzzles – Use switches, splitters, bridges, and more to guide your trains through clever routing challenges.

  • Strategic Yet Relaxing – Thoughtful puzzles that challenge your brain without pressure. Just good vibes.

  • Create and Share with the Level Editor – Build your own cozy conundrums and explore puzzles from the community.

  • Collect and Customize – Unlock a variety of adorable engines and cars inspired by classic toys as you flip through the toy catalogue.

  • Nostalgic, Diorama-Style Environments – Each level is a love letter to childhood spaces, filled with cozy detail.

  • Play the Traincade! – Visit the in-game retro arcade cabinet packed with train-themed twists on classic games. Yes, Train Invaders is a thing, you’re welcome. 

It’s a game built on memories, the click of a wooden track on a carpet, the excitement of circling your dream engine in a toy catalog, the glow of an old arcade screen with your favorite game ready to go.  It’s a cozy, handcrafted world that invites you to slow down, think creatively, and find joy in making things work, one tiny train at a time.
