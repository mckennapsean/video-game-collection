Description

Roombo: First Blood , the prequel to JUSTICE SUCKS, is a comedic, top-down, stealth-action game about being a robot vacuum cleaner and fending off burglars from your home.










Hack smart-home gadgets like lights, doors and various appliances to set up traps, or improvise weapons from objects around the house. Hide under furniture, set your trajectory and plow into your enemies, munching them up once they’re knocked down.

Make sure you don’t leave too much blood on the carpet; you’ll need to make sure the house is clean before the family gets home. Only you can save Christmas and show these burglars that justice sucks!

This is a short experimental game featuring 6 difficulty scenarios set in one stage and an unlockable gallery of concept art.
